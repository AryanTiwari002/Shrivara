Our Heritage & Atelier

The Art of Indian Royalty, Reimagined for Modern Life

"Jewellery is not merely an ornament; it is an enduring expression of Indian heritage, personal grace, and celebratory spirit."

Shrivara was founded with a singular conviction: that the regal grandeur of Indian heritage jewellery should be accessible without exorbitant markups or fleeting durability. Situated in Howrah — world-renowned for master gold karigars — each piece is handcrafted in pure brass alloy and sealed with our signature electro-chemical vacuum micro-layer.

Whether you are preparing for a wedding celebration or wearing everyday elegance, our collections are formulated to withstand humidity, sweat, and festive wear while preserving royal mirror lustre.
