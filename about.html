OUR STORY & ATELIER

Born from the heritage jewellery corridors of Bengal, Shrivara bridges royal Indian traditions and modern everyday luxury with our signature anti-tarnish micro-layer craft.

Home Our Story & Atelier
A Legacy of Elegance

Handcrafted with Devotion in Howrah, West Bengal

Shrivara was founded with a singular purpose: to bring the breathtaking grandeur of Indian heritage jewellery into modern wardrobes without the prohibitive price tag or fleeting durability of conventional fashion jewellery.

Situated in Howrah — world-renowned for its master gold karigars and metal smiths — every Shrivara piece is shaped by generational artisans who treat imitation craftsmanship with the exact reverence, weight balance, and prong precision of fine jewellery.

From majestic Hazoorilal-inspired bridal chokers to delicate contemporary AD tops, each design honors individuality and celebration.

Engineering Brilliance

The Anti-Tarnish Micro-Layer Innovation

Traditional artificial jewellery often discolors, turns dark, or leaves green marks on the skin after coming in contact with Indian humidity, festive perfumes, or sweat.

At Shrivara, we resolved this with our signature Anti-Tarnish Vacuum Micro-Layer Coating. Each brass alloy base receives multi-stage copper and nickel-free gold dipping, sealed by an invisible hydrophobic barrier that resists oxidation and preserves high mirror lustre for years.
